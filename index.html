Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is my data really private?

Yes. PDFs you open are read, filled and saved entirely in your browser on your computer. There is no server, no upload, and no analytics. The extension's only permission is local storage.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. You open a PDF and start filling.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

Basic form filling stays free forever. Saved profiles, smart refill, import and export, and advanced signing need a one-time $19 license. Your saved data is never deleted or held hostage.

What kinds of PDFs work?

Standard fillable PDF forms (the AcroForm kind) get the full experience. Flat or scanned pages can still be signed, and you can place typed text anywhere on them. Owner-protected government forms are unlocked on your device. Password-locked and XFA files get a clear explanation instead of a broken download.

Which browsers does it support?

Chrome and other Chromium browsers - Edge, Brave, and similar - installed from the Chrome Web Store.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is one $19 payment, ever. Restore purchase brings your license back on any machine you use.
